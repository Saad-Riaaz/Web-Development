Kohat University of Science and Technology (KUST) is a premier public sector university located in the Kohat District of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. Established in 2001 by the then-governor Lt. Gen. Syed Iftikhar Hussain Shah, the university has evolved into a vital hub for research, academic excellence, and regional development. Spanning a modern 300-acre main campus, KUST offers a student-centered learning environment serving over 8,000 students with an expansive range of undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs. Academically, it features prominent faculties such as Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences, Biological Sciences, Management Sciences, Social Sciences, and Physical & Numerical Sciences
